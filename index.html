Tokyo

From the bustling streets of Shibuya and the neon lights of Shinjuku to the peaceful gardens of the Imperial Palace, Tokyo offers something for everyone. Whether you're a foodie eager to explore Michelin-starred restaurants and ramen stalls or a culture enthusiast fascinated by cutting-edge technology and ancient traditions, Tokyo promises an unforgettable experience at every turn.

    Must-Sees;
  • Shibuya Crossing – The world's busiest pedestrian crossing
  • Shinjuku Gyoen – A peaceful park with beautiful gardens
  • Senso-ji Temple – Tokyo’s oldest and most famous temple in Asakusa

Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan’s cultural heart, famous for its ancient temples, serene Zen gardens, and traditional tea houses. Explore the majestic Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion, as it glistens in the sunlight, reflected in the surrounding pond. Wander the stone pathways of Higashiyama, lined with centuries-old wooden buildings. Kyoto’s timeless charm offers a peaceful retreat from the modern world, inviting visitors to step back in time and immerse themselves in Japan’s rich heritage.

    Must-Sees;
  • Fushimi Inari Shrine – The iconic path of thousands of red torii gates
  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) – A stunning gold-leaf-covered Zen temple
  • Gion District – Kyoto’s famous geisha district with traditional tea houses

Mt.Fuji

Mount Fuji, Japan’s tallest and most iconic mountain, is a must-see for visitors. During the climbing season in summer, thousands of hikers ascend its trails to witness the breathtaking sunrise from the peak, a phenomenon known as Goraiko. Surrounding the mountain, the Fuji Five Lakes region offers stunning views, hot springs, and traditional ryokan inns for those seeking relaxation. Whether you seek adventure, serenity, or spiritual reflection, Mount Fuji provides an unforgettable experience.

    Must-Sees;
  • Pagoda for the perfect Fuji view
  • Fuji Five Lakes (Lake Kawaguchi is the most popular)
  • Oshino Hakkai – traditional village with clear spring water ponds

Hokkaido

Hokkaido, Japan’s northernmost island, is a paradise for nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, Hokkaido offers vast national parks, rugged coastlines, and some of the best hot springs in the country. Hokkaido’s fresh seafood, especially its crab and sea urchin, is another highlight, making it a must-visit destination for food lovers. Whether you seek adventure, relaxation, or unique cultural experiences, Hokkaido’s untouched beauty will leave a lasting impression.

    Must-Sees;
  • Sapporo Snow Festival – giant ice sculptures in February
  • Furano’s lavender fields – stunning in summer
  • Noboribetsu Onsen – famous for its volcanic hot springs

Osaka

Osaka, Japan’s kitchen, is a city renowned for its vibrant nightlife, rich history, and mouthwatering street food. Explore the bustling streets of Dotonbori, where neon lights reflect over the canal, and try Osaka’s famous takoyaki (octopus balls) and okonomiyaki (savoury pancakes).Whether you’re shopping in the vibrant district of Shinsaibashi or enjoying the city’s unique charm, Osaka’s lively energy makes it an unmissable destination.

    Must-Sees;
  • Dotonbori – The heart of Osaka’s nightlife and street food scene
  • Osaka Castle – A historic landmark surrounded by lush gardens
  • Kuromon Ichiba Market – A paradise for food lovers
